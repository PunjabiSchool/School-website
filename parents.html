Welcome Parents

Welcome to the Gurdwara Guru Granth Sahib Punjabi School Parents' Hub. This page has been created for parents to give their feedback and suggestions to improve Punjabi School.

We greatly value the continued support of our parents and families. Working together helps our children develop confidence in speaking, reading and writing Punjabi while strengthening their understanding of Sikh heritage, culture and values.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the Punjabi School?

Children from Nursery age through to adults are welcome.

When are classes held?

Every Sunday Morning Session 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM Afternoon Session 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM

Which examination board do you use?

We are an approved AQA Examination Centre offering GCSE and A Level Punjabi.

Can parents stay during Nursery classes?

Yes. Parents may be asked to remain nearby while younger learners settle into class.
