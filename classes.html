Our Classes

Learning Punjabi from Nursery through to GCSE and A Level

Welcome to Our Classes

At Gurdwara Guru Granth Sahib Punjabi School, Albert Drive, children and adults are given the opportunity to learn the Punjabi language, the language in which the writings of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji are based.

Our teaching is structured to support students in developing their Punjabi language skills and progressing towards GCSE and A-Level qualifications. Our students have achieved excellent results, with many attaining high grades.

Classes are taught by committed volunteer teachers in purpose-built classrooms, providing a welcoming environment where learners can develop their Punjabi language skills and understanding.

Learning at Our Punjabi School

Classrooms

Children develop their Punjabi language skills through listening, speaking, reading and writing.

Learning Punjabi

Learners develop their understanding of the Punjabi alphabet and Gurmukhi script alongside vocabulary, reading and communication skills.

Achievement & Progress

We celebrate the achievements of our learners and support them as they progress through their Punjabi learning journey.

Our Approach

Our classes introduces young learners to Punjabi through games, songs, stories, colouring activities and simple conversation. The aim is to make learning Punjabi enjoyable and help children develop confidence from an early age.

Who Can Attend?

Our school welcomes learners from the age of 4 onwards, with no upper age limit. We provide opportunities for children, young people and adults to learn and develop their Punjabi language skills.
