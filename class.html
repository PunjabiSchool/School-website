About Our Punjabi School

Serving the Glasgow community and promoting Punjabi language, Sikh heritage and culture. It is important for us to learn Punjabi because Punjabi is our mother tongue as well as the language of the Sikh religion.

Through learning Punjabi, we gain an understanding of Sikh cultural and spiritual heritage and develop a strong connection with Punjab, the birthplace of Sikhism.

More importantly, Guru Granth Sahib Ji is written in Gurmukhi. By learning Punjabi, children and adults are able to read, understand and participate more fully within the Sangat.

Our aim is to provide a high-quality curriculum that enables learners to develop confidence in speaking, reading and writing Punjabi, progressing from Nursery through to GCSE and A Level Punjabi qualifications.
