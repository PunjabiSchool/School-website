Virsa – Annual Punjabi School Programme

Learning, celebrating and connecting with our Punjabi heritage

About Virsa

Virsa is our annual Punjabi School programme, which was started in 2023. It gives our children an opportunity to learn, participate and have fun while connecting with Punjabi language, culture, traditions and heritage.

Over the past three years, Virsa has been enjoyed and supported by our pupils and parents. It has become a special part of our Punjabi School calendar and gives children an opportunity to take what they learn in class and experience it in a fun and practical way.

Learning Through Culture

Virsa is one of the ways in which our children can learn about their culture outside the classroom.

Through activities, performances and participation, children can develop their confidence while learning more about Punjabi language, traditions, history and heritage.

Most importantly, it gives our children the opportunity to learn and have fun together.

Bringing Our Community Together

Virsa is not something that can happen without the support of our parents, teachers, volunteers and Gurdwara Sahib.

The support and involvement of our wider community helps make the programme possible and gives our children a positive experience that they can remember and enjoy.
